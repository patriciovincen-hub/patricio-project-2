About KCC

Our history, identity, mission, and commitment to education.

A Legacy of Faith and Education

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. traces its beginnings to 1927. From its early years as a local educational institution, KCC has continued to serve generations of learners in Kabankalan City and nearby communities.

Guided by its Catholic identity and the motto “Teach Ye All Nations,” the College seeks to nurture students through quality and inclusive education, faith formation, research, and meaningful community engagement.

KCC aims to form graduates who combine knowledge, character, and competence and who use their abilities for the good of society.
