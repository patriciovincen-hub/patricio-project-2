Welcome to KCC

“Teach Ye All Nations” — Matthew 28:19

Founded in 1927, Kabankalan Catholic College has been part of the educational history of Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental. The College continues its mission of forming learners who are knowledgeable, competent, faithful, and ready to serve their communities.

  • Academic excellence and lifelong learning
  • Catholic values and faith formation
  • Community engagement and service
